How an AI Chooses the Next Word

When an AI writes a sentence, it scores thousands of possible words. Parameters like Temperature, Top-K, and Top-P control which words get picked!

🔥 Temperature
What it does: Controls how adventurous or unpredictable the AI is.
• Low Temp (0.1): Safe & repetitive. AI almost always picks the #1 top word.
• High Temp (1.5): Wild & creative. Spreads scores out so rare words can win!
Rule: Boosts or levels out word scores
📊 Top-K
What it does: A strict limit on the number of allowed candidate words.
• Top-K = 3: Only the top 3 highest-scoring words are kept. All other words are thrown straight into the trash bin!
Rule: Hard cap on total candidate words
🎯 Top-P (Nucleus)
What it does: A dynamic confidence target (e.g., 90%).
• Keeps adding top words until their total probability adds up to P%, then chops off the rest.
• Automatically shrinks when AI is sure, expands when AI is unsure!
Rule: Target cumulative percentage cutoff
